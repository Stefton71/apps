Privacy policy

Apri P7M – Digital Signature for Mac, iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short

Apri P7M does not collect, store or transmit personal data. The documents you open are processed only on your device.

Documents

The files you open and the documents extracted from them stay on your device. Extracted documents are kept in a temporary app folder that is emptied when the app starts and quits, unless you save them yourself with Save As or Share.

Network use

The app connects to the Internet only in the two cases below:

  • Updating the lists of trust service providers: once a week at launch (you can turn this off in Settings), or when you click “Update Lists”, the app downloads the public lists of qualified trust service providers from the European Commission (ec.europa.eu) and from the national authorities' websites listed there. None of your data is sent.
  • Checking revocation status: if you turn it on in Settings, the operating system queries the OCSP or CRL service of the provider that issued the signer's certificate. The provider receives only the certificate's identifying data (for example its serial number), never the document.

Data collected

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking and requires no account. Preferences (for example whether revocation checking is on) and the list of recent files (only name, result and a reference to reopen them, never the content; can be turned off) are saved locally on your device.

Purchases

The app is free and contains no in-app purchases or advertising.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data about visits (such as the IP address) under its own privacy policy. The site uses no cookies or analytics; your chosen language is remembered only in your browser.
