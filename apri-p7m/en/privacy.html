Privacy policy

Apri P7M – Digital Signature for macOS · Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short

Apri P7M does not collect, store or transmit personal data. The documents you open are processed only on your Mac.

Documents

The files you open and the documents extracted from them stay on your Mac. Extracted documents are kept in a temporary app folder that is emptied when the app starts and quits, unless you save them yourself with Save As.

Network use

The app connects to the Internet only in the two cases below, both at your request:

  • Updating the list of trust service providers: when you click «Aggiorna da AgID» (Update from AgID) in Settings, the app downloads the public list of qualified trust service providers from eidas.agid.gov.it. None of your data is sent.
  • Checking revocation status: if you turn it on in Settings, macOS queries the OCSP or CRL service of the provider that issued the signer's certificate. The provider receives only the certificate's identifying data (for example its serial number), never the document.

Data collected

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking and requires no account. The only preferences (for example whether revocation checking is on) are saved locally on your Mac.
