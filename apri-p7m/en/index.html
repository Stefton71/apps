Apri P7M – Digital Signature

Open .p7m and .p7s files and signed PDFs, verify the digital signature and read the document. Verification happens entirely on your Mac.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store · Free, no ads · macOS 14 or later · English, Italiano, Deutsch, Français, Español

Checks performed

  • Integrity: the document has not been modified since it was signed.
  • Chain of trust: the certificate was issued by a qualified trust service provider on the eIDAS trusted lists of every EU and EEA country.
  • Qualified certificate and qualified signature creation device (QSCD).
  • Certificate validity at the time of the timestamp, or at today's date.
  • Timestamp verified and used as the time reference.
  • Later changes: for PDFs, the app tells you if the document was modified after signing and shows the signed version.
  • Revocation status (OCSP/CRL), if you turn it on in Settings.

Features

  • Signed PDFs (PAdES and PKCS#7), including multiple signatures and document timestamps.
  • Multiple signatures (.pdf.p7m.p7m) and multiple signers.
  • Detached signatures (.p7s) together with the original document.
  • Files in binary, Base64 or PEM format.
  • Quick Look preview, opening in the default app, and Save As.
  • Recognises PDFs, Italian XML e-invoices, Office documents and images.
  • Electronic invoices in readable form (lines, VAT, payment, attachments), including unsigned XML.
  • Timestamped .tsd and .m7m files: signature and timestamp verification.
  • Print or save as PDF the invoice and the verification report.
  • Quick Look preview in the Finder (space bar) and recent files.
  • Verification report you can copy.
  • Interface in English, Italian, German, French and Spanish, following your Mac's language.

Privacy

The app collects no data and never sends your documents over the Internet. Read the privacy policy.

The verification result is for information only and does not replace the qualified validation services defined by the eIDAS Regulation.
