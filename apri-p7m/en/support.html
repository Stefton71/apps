Frequently asked questions

Why is the signature shown as intact but with warnings?

The document has not been modified, but at least one certificate check failed or was not performed: for example revocation checking is off, the certificate has expired and there is no timestamp, or the provider is not on the European qualified lists.

The certificate has expired: is the signature valid?

Without a timestamp there is no proof that the signature was made while the certificate was valid. If the signature contains a recognised timestamp, the app checks the certificate at the time of the timestamp.

How do I verify a .p7s file?

A .p7s file contains only the signature: open the .p7s and choose the original document when the app asks for it.

Can I verify signed PDFs (PAdES)?

Yes: open the PDF like any other file. The app verifies every signature and timestamp in the PDF and tells you if the document was modified after signing; “Signed Version” shows the PDF exactly as it was when signed.

The trust service provider is not recognised.

The European lists of qualified providers update automatically every week; to download them now, open Settings and click “Update Lists”.

How do I open an .xml.p7m electronic invoice?

Open it like any other file: the app verifies the signature and shows the invoice in readable form, with lines, VAT, payment details and attachments, which you can print or save as PDF. You can save the XML file or open it with your invoicing software. Unsigned .xml invoices open too.

Can I open .tsd and .m7m files?

Yes: the app verifies the enclosed signature and the timestamp, which proves that the document existed at the time of timestamping.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Italian, German, French and Spanish: the app automatically uses your device's language. You can choose another one on the Mac in System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications, and on iPhone and iPad in Settings → Apri P7M → Language.

How do I open a .p7m received by email on iPhone?

Tap the attachment: the preview immediately shows the document and the signature result. For the full report, tap Share → Apri P7M. In Outlook, tap the attachment, then “Open In…” → Apri P7M.

How much does it cost?

Nothing: the app is free, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

What legal value does the result have?

It is an informational check performed on your device and does not replace the qualified validation services defined by the eIDAS Regulation.
