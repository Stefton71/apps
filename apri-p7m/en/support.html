Frequently asked questions

Why is the signature shown as intact but with warnings?

The document has not been modified, but at least one certificate check failed or was not performed: for example revocation checking is off, the certificate has expired and there is no timestamp, or the provider is not on the AgID qualified list.

The certificate has expired: is the signature valid?

Without a timestamp there is no proof that the signature was made while the certificate was valid. If the signature contains a recognised timestamp, the app checks the certificate at the time of the timestamp.

How do I verify a .p7s file?

A .p7s file contains only the signature: open the .p7s and choose the original document when the app asks for it.

Can I verify signed PDFs (PAdES)?

Yes: open the PDF like any other file. The app verifies every signature and timestamp in the PDF and tells you if the document was modified after signing; “Signed Version” shows the PDF exactly as it was when signed.

The trust service provider is not recognised.

Open Settings and click “Update from AgID” to download the latest list of qualified providers.

How do I open an .xml.p7m electronic invoice?

Open it like any other file: the app verifies the signature and extracts the invoice XML file, which you can save or open with your invoicing software.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Italian, German, French and Spanish: the app automatically uses your Mac's language. You can choose another one in System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications.

What legal value does the result have?

It is an informational check performed on your Mac and does not replace the qualified validation services defined by the eIDAS Regulation.
